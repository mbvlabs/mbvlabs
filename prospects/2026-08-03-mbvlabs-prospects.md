## Search coverage and limitations

- Coverage: 45 MBV Labs API search requests across funding, launches, technical hiring, integrations, automation, AI platforms, and regional startup activity. Searches used English in the United States and England, plus Danish and English in Denmark.
- Research volume: 373 unique result URLs were returned. Twenty-one named companies entered targeted verification, and 52 individual public pages were scraped for the strongest candidates.
- Market balance: Five qualified prospects each in the United States, Denmark, and England. England locations were checked as London or Cambridge, not elsewhere in the United Kingdom.
- Main rejection reasons: unclear geography, teams too large, no official evidence, no current pressure, insufficient buyer access, stealth status, or weak fit with hands-on backend and platform delivery. Payna, Franklin, and OpenHuman were among the researched companies not used in the final 15 because stronger market-matched evidence was available.
- Size limitation: Several early-stage companies do not publish headcount. They are marked Unknown and receive only five size points rather than an inferred ideal-size score.
- Contact limitation: Only public professional routes were recorded. No email patterns were guessed, no restricted platform was scraped, and no deliverability, consent, or buying intent is claimed.
- Manual confirmation: Recheck headcount, role tenure, funding details, and contact routes before any outreach. Denmark requires consent or relationship review. England requires UK GDPR and PECR review even where the company appears to be a corporate subscriber. United States sending must satisfy CAN-SPAM requirements.
